The new single 'Say', taken from the new Album 'Genuflector' is out now on digital

"Say is a breakup song in disguise. Choral grandeur meets with bluegrass folk rhythms and pop melodies. It's a 'why not' record in any ways - that means that it has no agenda and was written in a kind of 'one take' kind of way. I hope this record is not just 'pleasant' for listeners, but also touching and exciting at the same time. I'd call 'Say' a homage to relationships struggles and resolution. This record posits: "You can find a your way through love, devotion, lust and obsession with someone by listening to spirits guides and ancestral powers".
