‘Messages, the second single from My upcoming album ‘Genuflector’, is out now digitally and available to pre-order today. 

‘Genuflector’ will be released on 8 March on streaming, CD, and standard vinyl, along with grey and clear limited edition vinyl available to pre-order now here.

“The music has often been described as "emotionally loaded". Loaded things can be pretty heavy to bare and when listening to 'messages' I feel this heavy groove. It's not a pleasant trip. I hope this record effects the listener as profoundly as it has me. It's as if the making of this record maintained the attitude of - "I'm not holding back on this one". It's a Love song, of sorts. When I listen to this record, I don't know, I guess I'm happy. I'm reaching for the replay button.
