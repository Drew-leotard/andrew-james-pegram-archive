‘Messages, the second single from My upcoming album ‘Genuflector’, is out now digitally and available to pre-order today. 

‘Genuflector’ will be released on 8 March on streaming, CD, and standard vinyl, along with grey and clear limited edition vinyl available to pre-order now here.

“I hope this album speak to you as loudly as it speaks to me. I've been through contrasting emotions with it. There's been times when I wasn't sure if this album would ever be ready. There's no roadmap to releasing an album and I hope over the coming years to release many more. When I listen to this record I feel a sense of wonder! It's as if I'm never quite sure where it all came from and how it go out. Am I happy? Yes, I'm happy.”
