Georg Kostron lined up to collaborate on new single 'Messages'.

Bass is an incredible frequency range. Low, full, steady energy is integral to the music I make. Full, melodic, supportive, primal and even fundamental in terms of tactility - 'in' and 'with' body listening and experiencing of musical sonic art forms. 

When creative producer and Famous Gold Watch studio owner and founder Cameron James Laing suggested hiring Georg Kostron as a session musician for 'Stole You From The Underworld' I went along perfectly trusting that, what would eventually step into the bass role of that song, would be nothing short of magnificent. 

As the follow up recording to 'Stole You From The Underworld', 'Messages' assumes a lightly submissive role. Not dominated by layered tracks of lush strings, pianos, organic sound design atmospheres or 10 piece choirs, this composition leaves room for the aforementioned, more primal constellation, namely - DRUMS and BASS. 

Georg lives and works in Berlin, Germany and is, to my mind, one of the few technically and creatively brilliant bass players around. Follow him here
