Stole You From The Underworld entered into the International Songwriting Contest 2026, and it feels like.. 'organic energy', man.

After receiving a newsletter from my royalty collections society PRS, I happened across a link to the competition and, to my astonishment, immediately felt a pang of inspiration. Lo and behold, the deadline for submissions had been extended and that deadline gave me ample room to finish up some final mix teaks at my home studio in Leipzig.

As far as competitions go I'm often skeptical, especially when charged an entry fee. Still, something organic had brought me there and I've acted on impulses like these in the past only to watch them come up trumps. I dug deep and forked out 27 quid, uploaded a 320kbps mp3 file of the latest mix and hit 'submit'.

I imagine the song out there somewhere - naked, exposed and burning in the spotlights of these plush industry swagger-dens. Queued up behind a trillion other playlist entries, in limbo and yet, seasoned enough to stay its course. 

The board of listeners aka panel of judges is adorned with a myriad of A&R bods, label giants and music execs, but the one name that caught my eye was Mr Tom Waites, an artist and human being I admire greatly. I can imagine his ears could be right on the very category I've selected for 'Stole You From The Underworld' - Folk/singer/songwriter. Tom Waites may very well listen to, and get a tickle for, this song.

Semi finalists will be selected February 2027 and Winners will be announced around April 2027.
