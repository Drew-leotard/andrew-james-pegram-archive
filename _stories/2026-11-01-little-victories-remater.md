Beginnings:

In November 2010 I landed in Berlin and proceeded to David Bowie and Iggy Pop's old stomping ground 'Schöneberg', where over the phone I'd negotiated with a delightful 'Wolfgang' tenancy of a large 36sqm room. It was here, within the walls of this skateboard and skateboard accessories factory my first Berlin studio arose, and with it my debut album 'Little Victories' 

This seminal debut album is set to be remixed, remastered and re-released on limited edition 12" Vinyl, digital and here exclusively on the map of Epochs. All 9 songs have been drawn from the depths of a now swelling archive and will be taken to various studios and mix locations across Manchester and the North of England.
